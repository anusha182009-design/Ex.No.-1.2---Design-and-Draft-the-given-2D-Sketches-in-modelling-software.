## EX.NO.-1.2-DESIGN AND DRAFT THE GIVEN 2D SKETCHES IN MODELLING SOFTWARE. 

## AIM:

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool. 

## REQUIREMENT: 
1.Autodesk fusion 360.                                                                                          
2.Windows 10.                                                                                                              
3.16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)                                                                       
4.2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE: 
1.Open your fusion 360 tool. Select the “New part design”   
2.Open new file and then select create sketch icon.

<img width="481" height="322" alt="create sketch" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c2c6e894-e9cc-4517-afc9-06ba6284b5b4" />

3.Select the plane for sketching.    
4.Select the line to draw.

<img width="470" height="347" alt="line" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ebee764-362f-4ea7-a309-c4ba2b76be17" />

5.Draw a line of 60mm above the origin and again draw a line of 50mm on positive x-axis.                                                           
6.Draw a 20mm line at the end of the 50mm line.                                                       
7.Then select the circle to sketch. 

<img width="490" height="263" alt="circle" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1964d242-e84a-44af-a77b-9524e1c72f7f" />

8.Draw four circles on the origin of radius 16mm,21mm,32mm,and 40mm respectively.                                  
9.Draw a 3mm line on the 32mm circle and again draw a perpendicular line to the circle.                                      
10.Using the trim option, trim the unwanted part. 

<img width="492" height="307" alt="trim" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5a40e14d-de92-45ba-bbcd-084aeb8f04d8" />

11. Using the mirror option, mirror the line..Click the mirror option and then select the 
objects and center line and mirror it.

<img width="452" height="146" alt="mirror" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8a96b84f-c784-455a-aef8-a37102d342dd" />

12..Use circular pattern to draw more. In circular select the object and center point and the 
number the circles as 6. 

<img width="487" height="210" alt="circle pattern" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/71aa7e8d-8a5b-4883-beb4-de620ccfdfa4" />

13.Draw an outer and inner circle of radius 15mm and 21mm on the end of the 50mm line.                                                  
14.Draw an outer and inner circle of radius 15mm and 20mm on the end of the 20mm line.                                                 
15.Draw a 20mm radius of circle on the positive x-axis.                                                                                 
16.Tangent it with 40mm radius of  circle and 21mm radius circle and trim it the extra part.                                           
17.Draw a line and coincident with the 21mm and 20mm radius of circles.                                                                
18.Then mirror the circles and lines.                                                                                                   
19.Trim the unwanted shapes and lines.                                                                                             
20.After finishing the sketch use the finish sketch option to finalize the sketch.

<img width="470" height="216" alt="Finish sketch" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6be74b98-9beb-4997-9394-511b8d2ae991" />

21.Then select extrude and give the distance as 20.

<img width="465" height="245" alt="Extr" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/af4adf17-8103-4bb0-b3b4-32898c0abb8f" />

22.After finishing the sketch switch to drawing mode to plot the sketch in ISO standard. 

<img width="282" height="358" alt="disign" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/44cb2320-ed66-4f1f-83d5-fc2cba8e32a6" />

23..Then select the manual and give it ok. 

<img width="287" height="310" alt="manual" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6d4b1c19-66f0-445e-a7d2-24c9b96a24dd" />

24. Place the object in the center and mark the linear,radius and diameter using their 
respective dimensions.
